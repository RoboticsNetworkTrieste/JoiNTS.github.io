Every section on the site opens with one of these — never a bare `<h2>`.

```jsx
<SectionHeading kicker="Progetti" title="Poche cose, fatte fino in fondo."
  sub="Scegliamo con cura i progetti su cui investire le nostre energie e li portiamo a termine." />
```

Titles are statements, often lifted from the manifesto. Left-aligned by default; centre only for full-width call-to-action bands.
