Photography of real hardware is the loudest element in this brand — this is how it gets framed. Cool, slightly desaturated, always annotated.

```jsx
<ImagePlate ratio="21 / 9" bleed index="FIG. 01"
  caption="Harbor 01 · Porto Vecchio · 2026-03-14" />

<ImagePlate src="/foto/braccio.jpg" alt="Braccio 01 in officina" ratio="4 / 5" index="FIG. 04" />
```

- Never place a graphic element or an illustration in a plate — plates are for photographs.
- Captions are mono uppercase and factual: subject · place · date. No adjectives.
- With no `src` the plate states that a photograph is missing rather than faking one.
