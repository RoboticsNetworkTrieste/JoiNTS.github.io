Every panel, tile and modal body in JoiNTS is a Card. Hairline border + 14px radius; shadows are reserved for floating layers.

```jsx
<Card>…</Card>
<Card variant="grid" pad="lg">…</Card>
<Card href="/progetti/harbor-slam" interactive>…</Card>
```

Interactive cards lift 2px and take an accent border on hover — never change their background to the accent.
